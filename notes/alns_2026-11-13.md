# ALNS on the initial solution (two-way check)

`alns.py`, called from `MultiEchelonPipeline.run_initial_construction` as step 1++
(`ALNS_INITIAL = True`, `ALNS_ITERATIONS = 1000`, `ALNS_TIME_LIMIT = 120` s) after the
relocate + 2-opt* descent and before the solution becomes a candidate / the MIP warm start.
Wrapper: `Multi_level_destroy.alns_initial` (same contract as `local_search_initial`).
The old `Solve_by_multi_level_ALNS` is left as it was (it is not called by the pipeline).

## Method

* **Cost** = two-way cost of every route (vehicle + c^s (distance + detours) + trip cost per trip,
  transshipments by `two_way_transshipment_checking`), the scale of `local_search.py` and
  `RH.two_way_solution_cost`. The search only visits feasible solutions.
* **Destroy** (q uniform in [5%, 30%] of the customers, at most 40): random, worst (distance
  saving, randomized p = 3), related (Shaw: distance, window opening, demand; p = 6), route
  (whole route, small ones more likely, topped up with its nearest customers), string
  (one string of consecutive customers per route around a seed and its neighbours). A partial
  route that fails the check again gives all its customers to the pool.
* **Repair**: greedy, greedy with noise, regret-2, regret-3, sequential by window opening.
  Per route the positions pass the O(1) forward / backward time screen of the local search; the
  3 cheapest by distance get the exact two-way check unless the local-search cost bound shows
  they cannot beat the best one found. Opening a new route is always an option.
* **Acceptance**: simulated annealing. Start temperature: 5% worse distance accepted with
  probability 1/2 (distance part of the cost only, so an extra vehicle or trip at 10000 is in
  effect never accepted); geometric cooling to 0.002 x start over iterations / time.
* **Adaptive weights**: segments of 50 iterations, scores 33 / 9 / 13, reaction 0.1.
* Every new global best goes through the relocate + 2-opt* descent (shared route cache).
  The 5 best distinct route sets are stored for `check_found_solutions`.
* **Speed-up** (`RH.satellite_detour`): the closest satellite of an arc is memoized per problem
  and satellite set; `refill_detours` and `get_makespan` use it. Same values, about 3x faster
  route checks.

## Results (initial stage, heuristic only, 2 starts, seed 32, `scripts/compare_best_known.py`)

Fleet = SEVs / FEVs / transshipments. Objective = 10000 per SEV, FEV and trip + distance.
"Best known, ours" = the best-known SEV routes through our two-echelon check (as in
`local_search_2026-11-13.md`).

The ALNS reaches or beats the best-known routes under our objective on 5 of 6 instances. It cuts
one SEV on c201, c208 and rc201. On r201, r208 and rc208 it keeps one SEV more than the best known
and saves one or two trips instead, which costs the same in our objective. The paper ranks the
fleet first, so against the paper's own ranking these three are still one SEV short. The ALNS
does not change the water level: the FE routes come from the water MIP of `check_found_solutions`.
